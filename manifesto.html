House Rules

We make logic puzzles that respect your time.

We are one person, working slowly, shipping small games to Android. This page explains what that means in practice.

01

Logic over luck

Every puzzle we publish has a single solution, reachable through logic alone. No guessing. No "lucky tap". If the only way to progress is to try and see, we did not finish designing the puzzle.

Your time should not be spent gambling against the level designer.
02

Offline first

You should be able to play on a plane, in a tunnel, in a campsite with no signal, with airplane mode on for the next eight hours. Our games work without an account, without a login wall, without a network call to "verify your session" before letting you tap a cell.

The internet is for downloading the app. After that, the game is yours.
03

No dark patterns

We will never use a timer to push you into an in-app purchase. We will never show you a fake "limited offer" that resets every Monday. We will never interrupt a puzzle you are solving with a popup for hints you do not need.

If there is an ad, it is between levels, capped, and labelled. If there is a "Remove Ads" button, one tap removes them forever. If you opt out of analytics in the settings, we stop collecting. The settings screen is not a maze.

We assume our players are adults and we behave accordingly.
04

Made for grown-ups

Our players are 30 to 55. They have jobs, kids, plants, mortgages, and roughly twenty minutes of quiet in the evening. They do not need a mascot to celebrate a completed level. They do not need a streak shaming them on Tuesday morning.

The reward for solving a puzzle is the puzzle being solved.

We are a small studio with a long backlog and no urgency. If this is the kind of game you have been looking for, we ship them on Google Play.
