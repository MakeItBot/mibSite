The .MIB mark

A two-tier identity: makeit.bot is the URL and full studio name; .MIB is the short mark — used for the app icon, favicon, social avatars and anywhere a small badge needs to read at a glance. The two work together as a lockup, or separately at scale.

App icon recommendation
  • Forest (option A) is the recommended primary — it matches the Game Boy landing palette, holds up at all Play Store sizes, and the yellow mark pops on every phone wallpaper.
  • Each individual game gets its own Play Store icon (Nonogram Zen has its own cover art). The .MIB icon is for the publisher / studio level — your launcher / dev page / web bookmark.
